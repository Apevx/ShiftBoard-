How it's calculated
  • Base rate = weekly rate ÷ 35 (Medical Radiation Scientists Award 2025). July 2026 rates apply from the first full pay period starting on or after 1 July 2026.
  • Mon–Fri shift allowance by start time: 10:00–12:59 10%, 13:00–15:59 12.5%, 16:00–03:59 15%, 04:00–05:59 10%.
  • Saturday hours ×1.5, Sunday ×1.75 (in place of shift allowance). NSW public holidays +150%.
  • Overtime ×1.5 for the first 2 h each day then ×2; Sunday ×2; public holiday ×2.5.
  • Called in: minimum 4 h at overtime rates. Remote recall: minimum 1 h.
  • On call: one allowance per 24 h or part — $31.45 if rostered on that day, $61.99 if rostered off (July 2026 rates).
  • Gross only — before tax, super and salary packaging. Check against your payslip.
